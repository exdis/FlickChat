Word list

The words in Words.lua come from SCOWL (the English Speller Database, https://wordlist.aspell.net, size 60,
US spelling) and Tatoeba's English sentences. The word and word-pair counts are derived from Tatoeba's
English sentences (https://tatoeba.org), licensed under CC BY 2.0 FR
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/).

The other languages' word lists

The words, word counts and word-pair counts in the language folders (FlickChat_ptBR, FlickChat_frFR, FlickChat_deDE,
FlickChat_itIT, FlickChat_esES) are derived from Tatoeba's Portuguese, French, German, Italian and Spanish sentences
(https://tatoeba.org), licensed under CC BY 2.0 FR (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/). Chat shorthand
and game words were added by hand; zone, dungeon, class and race names are the game's own.

Mature word filter

The words the mature word filter leaves out come from the English list of "List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and
Otherwise Bad Words" (https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words), licensed
under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: single words only, some everyday words left
out, plain endings and a few words added. The language folders' filters come from the same project's Portuguese,
French, German, Italian and Spanish lists, under the same license. Changes: single words only, some everyday words
left out.
